ABOUT SCC

A Ministry Built on Presence, Power, and Purpose

Supernatural City Church is an apostolic ministry committed to raising believers who walk in the supernatural reality of God's Kingdom. We are passionate about transforming lives through the ministry of the Word, Prayer, the Holy Spirit, and Kingdom Discipleship.

We exist to build people into Kings, Priests, and Prophets who carry God's Presence into every sphere of society and establish His influence across cities and nations.

OUR VISION

Establishing God's City in Every City

To raise Supernatural Christians — Kings, Priests, and Prophets — with a burning spirit to transform nations, by the Person, Presence, and Power of the Holy Spirit.

OUR MISSION

Three Commitments That Drive Everything We Do

MISSION

Aggressive Soul Winning

Relentlessly reaching the lost and building disciples who carry the fire of the gospel into their world.

MISSION

Advancing the Kingdom Supernaturally

Moving in the demonstration of the Spirit — signs, wonders, and power that establish Kingdom reality.

MISSION

Setting Hearts on Fire for God

Cultivating a burning, undivided devotion to Christ in every believer we raise.

OUR MANDATE

What We Have Been Called to Build

MANDATE

Raising Supernatural Christianity

Equipping believers to live and minister in the supernatural power of the Holy Spirit as a normal way of life.

MANDATE

Building God's City in Every City

Planting Kingdom influence and apostolic government in every city we are sent to.
