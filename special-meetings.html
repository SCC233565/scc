Signature Gatherings This Season

Beyond our weekly services, these gatherings are set apart for deeper impartation and encounter.

ANNUAL RETREAT

The Lions Retreat

A gathering set apart to raise fearless, fiery Kingdom leaders through impartation, deep teaching, and personal encounter with God. Lions Retreat is built for those who sense a call to lead — in ministry, business, or their sphere of influence — and want to be sharpened for it.

Expect focused sessions on leadership, character, and spiritual authority, alongside extended times of prayer and ministry.

CONVENTION

Signs and Wonders Convention

A convention dedicated to the demonstration of the Spirit's power in healing, deliverance, and miracles. This gathering exists to help believers move from hearing about the supernatural to walking in it personally.

Sessions include teaching on divine healing, activation times, and open ministry for those needing a breakthrough.

CONFERENCE

Prophetic Prayer Conference

Days set apart for prophetic activation, intercession, and hearing the voice of God clearly. Designed for believers who want to grow in sensitivity to the Holy Spirit and in the discipline of prevailing prayer.

Includes prophetic training sessions, corporate intercession, and one-on-one prophetic ministry.
