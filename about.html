ABOUT SCC

A Ministry Built on Presence, Power, and Purpose

Discover the vision, mission, and mandate driving everything we do at Supernatural City Church.

Supernatural City Church is an apostolic ministry committed to raising believers who walk in the supernatural reality of God's Kingdom. We are passionate about transforming lives through the ministry of the Word, Prayer, the Holy Spirit, and Kingdom Discipleship.

We exist to build people into Kings, Priests, and Prophets who carry God's Presence into every sphere of society and establish His influence across cities and nations. Every gathering, school, and outreach we run flows from one conviction: that ordinary people, filled with the Holy Spirit, become extraordinary carriers of God's glory.

From weekly services to prophetic schools and city-wide gatherings, Supernatural City Church exists as a spiritual headquarters — a place where the presence of God is tangible, the Word is taught with depth, and believers are sent out equipped to establish God's City wherever they are planted.

OUR VISION

Establishing God's City in Every City

To raise Supernatural Christians — Kings, Priests, and Prophets — with a burning spirit to transform nations, by the Person, Presence, and Power of the Holy Spirit.

OUR MISSION

Three Commitments That Drive Everything We Do

MISSION 01

Aggressive Soul Winning

Relentlessly reaching the lost and building disciples who carry the fire of the gospel into their world — in workplaces, campuses, and homes.

MISSION 02

Advancing the Kingdom Supernaturally

Moving in the demonstration of the Spirit — signs, wonders, and power that establish Kingdom reality, not just Kingdom theory.

MISSION 03

Setting Hearts on Fire for God

Cultivating a burning, undivided devotion to Christ in every believer we raise, so worship and obedience flow from love.

OUR MANDATE

What We Have Been Called to Build

MANDATE 01

Raising Supernatural Christianity

Equipping believers to live and minister in the supernatural power of the Holy Spirit as a normal way of life, not an occasional experience.

MANDATE 02

Building God's City in Every City

Planting Kingdom influence and apostolic government in every city we are sent to, through disciples who carry God's presence into culture.
