PASTOR

Pastor Grace Paul-Adeniran

Pastor Grace Paul-Adeniran serves alongside the vision of Supernatural City Church with a heart for discipleship, prayer, women's ministry, and raising godly families. Her ministry is marked by warmth, depth, and an unwavering commitment to seeing every woman she leads grow into her full Kingdom identity.

PASTORWOMEN'S MINISTRY LEADMENTORFAMILY ADVOCATE

Ministry Focus

Through teaching, mentoring, and spiritual leadership, Pastor Grace equips women to grow in intimacy with God and fulfill their Kingdom assignment. She leads with a conviction that discipleship happens best in relationship — not just from a stage — and invests deeply in the women God places in her care.

Her heart for godly families shapes much of her teaching, as she helps couples and single believers alike build homes that reflect Kingdom order and grace. She is a founding voice behind Watered Garden Mentorship, SCC's dedicated space for women to be discipled, nurtured, and released into purpose.

Serving Alongside the Vision

Pastor Grace works hand-in-hand with Apostle Samson Paul Adeniran to steward the vision of Supernatural City Church, bringing a pastoral, nurturing dimension to a ministry marked by apostolic fire. Together they model a partnership in ministry that many in the church look to as an example.
