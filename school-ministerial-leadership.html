SCC School of Ministerial Leadership

A structured training ground for those called to pastoral and ministry leadership.

The SCC School of Ministerial Leadership exists to train and equip believers who sense a call to ministry leadership — whether pastoral, departmental, or apostolic. The school combines sound biblical doctrine with practical ministry skill, so graduates leave not just informed, but able to actually lead, teach, and shepherd people well.
