Watered Garden Mentorship

A dedicated mentorship space for women — discipleship, identity, and purpose.

Watered Garden Mentorship is Supernatural City Church's dedicated mentorship platform for women, led by Pastor Grace Paul-Adeniran. Drawing on the image of a well-watered garden, the programme exists to help women grow deep roots in Christ, discover their identity, and flourish in their God-given purpose — as individuals, wives, mothers, and Kingdom leaders.
