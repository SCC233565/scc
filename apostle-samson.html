SET MAN & FOUNDER

Apostle Samson Paul Adeniran

Apostle Samson Paul Adeniran is the visionary leader and Set Man of Supernatural City Church, called to raise a generation of believers who walk in the tangible power and presence of God. His life and ministry are marked by a relentless pursuit of God's presence and a burning desire to see ordinary believers transformed into Kings, Priests, and Prophets.

APOSTLEREVIVALISTFOUNDER, SCCTEACHER OF THE WORD

Calling & Ministry

From the outset of his ministry, Apostle Samson Paul Adeniran carried a clear mandate: to raise supernatural Christianity and establish God's City in every city. This mandate shapes every service, school, and gathering at Supernatural City Church — nothing is done for tradition's sake; everything is built to position believers for genuine encounter with God.

His apostolic ministry is marked by revival, transformation, leadership development, and Kingdom advancement across nations. He is known for teaching the Word with clarity and depth while creating room for the Holy Spirit to move freely — believing that sound doctrine and supernatural power are meant to work together, not compete.

Ministry Philosophy

Apostle Samson teaches that every believer is called to function as a King, Priest, and Prophet — carrying dominion, intimacy with God, and prophetic insight into every sphere of life. This conviction is why Supernatural City Church invests heavily in schools, mentorship communities, and hands-on discipleship, not just weekly services.

He is passionate about raising leaders, not just gathering crowds — training men and women to carry their own weight in ministry, business, and society, carrying God's presence wherever they go.
