PROPHETIC TRAINING

School of the Prophets

Focused training in hearing God's voice accurately and walking in prophetic ministry.

The School of the Prophets trains believers to grow in the prophetic — not as a mystical add-on, but as a Spirit-empowered skill built on Scripture, character, and practice. Students are taught to hear God's voice with accuracy, deliver what they hear with wisdom, and carry prophetic ministry with integrity and honor.
